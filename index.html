A typical learning day

See it. Try it. Explain it.

The route is short and practical. Follow the steps in order, or jump straight to a lab that solves the problem you already have.

01 · NOTICEFind the problemSomething changes or fails.
02 · MODELName the piecesChoose the Workshop concepts.
03 · BUILDMake one changeKeep cause and effect visible.
04 · RUNObserve behaviorLet the system produce evidence.
05 · INSPECTVisualize the resultFind the relevant signal.
06 · EXPLAINState the limitSay what it proves and what it doesn't.
